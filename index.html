Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Where do you ship from, and how long does it take?

From the UK, with tracking on dispatch. Delivery timescales are shown at checkout, and we'd rather state a realistic one than an optimistic one.

Can I return something?

Yes. As a consumer you have 14 days from receipt to cancel most orders under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013, plus your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 if an item is faulty or not as described.

Is the item in stock?

If it's listed as in stock, it is. If we're waiting on it we say so rather than taking payment and emailing you about a delay later.

Will this storage box fit my cupboard?

Dimensions are on every listing. Measure first, and if you're unsure send us the measurements and we'll tell you honestly.

Do you sell to businesses?

Yes, and we'll quote for bulk or repeat orders. Note that the consumer 14-day cancellation right applies to consumers rather than business purchases.

Where do I see prices?

On our shop at [YOUR SHOP LINK]. Delivery is shown before you pay, not added at the end.

Not sure it's the right one?

Ask before you order. We'd rather answer a question than process a return.
